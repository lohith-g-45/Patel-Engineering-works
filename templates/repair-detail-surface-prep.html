Surface Preparation Services

Class-ready blasting and hydro-jetting for Marine surfaces - the foundation for lasting protection.

Proper Surface Preparation is key to long-lasting Vessel protection. Our treatment capabilities include a full range of blasting and hydro-jetting methods to meet classification and OEM surface standards.

Treatment Capabilities

  • Grit Blasting: Mechanical cleaning for effective surface roughening, achieving Sa 2.5 standards.
  • Vacuum Blasting: Minimized airborne dust for cleaner, blast-profiled surfaces in confined areas.
  • UHP Hydro Blasting: Ultra-High Pressure (up to 40,000 PSI) coating removal without abrasives.
  • Vacuum Shot Blasting: Specialized for flight decks and high-specification Naval surface requirements.
  • HP Hydro Jetting: High-pressure water jetting for pipe internal cleaning and tank scaling removal.

Quality Standards

All Surface Preparation is performed in accordance with SSPC, NACE, and classification society standards. Surface profiles are verified by certified inspectors prior to the application of any coating system.
