As an experienced marine engineering contractor, Patel Engineering Works (Vizag) specializes in the systematic renewal and structural repair of corroded or damaged ship steelwork—both afloat and in drydock conditions.

Structural Scope

We carry out extensive steel renewal for hull plating, internal bulkheads, tank structures, deck houses, and other key load-bearing and watertight components. Our services are tailored to meet the demanding requirements of naval and commercial platforms, where precision, speed, and classification compliance are non-negotiable.

Certified Technical Teams

Our steel renewal teams are composed of highly skilled fabricators and ABS/IRS-certified welders, proficient in working under tight schedules, complex geometries, and onboard constraints. All work is performed in strict adherence to approved Welding Procedure Specifications (WPS), with materials sourced as per IACS, Indian Navy, or client-specific standards.

Advanced Welding Techniques

We are experienced in the application of a wide range of advanced welding techniques, including:

  • FCAW (Flux-Cored Arc Welding)
  • SMAW (Shielded Metal Arc Welding)
  • GMAW (Gas Metal Arc Welding)
  • GTAW (Gas Tungsten Arc Welding)

Specialized Materials

We routinely work with a broad mix of marine-grade materials, including IS 2062 for standard steel renewal, AH36 for high-strength applications, and DMR 249A for naval-grade requirements. Our teams are also well-versed in fabricating and repairing structures made from aluminium alloy H8053 H111, commonly used for lightweight naval superstructures.

Our stringent QA/QC protocols are overseen by NDT Level II certified inspectors who ensure weld integrity and dimensional conformity at every stage of renewal.

As an experienced marine-engineering contractor, Patel Engineering Works (Vizag) specializes in the comprehensive overhaul and testing of hydraulic systems on naval and commercial vessels—including steering gear, deck machinery (winches, cranes), bow and stern doors, and specialized torpedo-launching mechanisms.

Technical Scope

Our scope covers high-pressure hydraulic lines, cylinders, valves, pumps, motors, and control systems—each subjected to pressure testing, full-system flushing, and leak-detection protocols to guarantee operational integrity. We also bench-test pumps and motors post-overhaul to verify performance before reinstallation.

Precision Restoration

Our technicians replace flexible hoses and fabricate piping assemblies with classification-approved fittings, adhering to ISO 28521 cleanliness standards and marine-grade corrosion requirements. Hydraulic cylinders are either reconditioned—via precision grinding, seal renewal, and hard-chrome plating—or manufactured new to OEM specifications, restoring dimensional accuracy and extending service life.

Rigorous QA/QC

We perform hydrostatic tests at 1.5 × design pressure, functional valve calibration, and final system tuning to confirm leak-free operation and compliance with OEM and class-society standards.

We deliver a focused suite of services for the propulsion and steering (stern gear equipment) of naval and commercial vessels—ensuring smooth maneuverability and long-term reliability.

Core Capabilities

  • Routine Maintenance & Overhaul: Scheduled servicing of steering systems, rudder assemblies, and complete shaft lines.
  • Heavy Lift Operations: Custom lifting frames and mobile crane operations safely extract/reinstall heavy assemblies.
  • Laser Shaft Alignment: Precision verification of sightline trueness from main engine output to propeller flange within stringent tolerances.
  • Propulsion Systems: Fixed Pitch or Controllable Pitch (CPP), Azimuth/Tunnel thrusters, and Waterjets.
  • Deep Seal Systems: Inspection and component-level or complete replacement of seal arrangements.
  • Gearboxes & Clutches: Overhaul and repair of reduction gearboxes, clutches, PTO units, and FiFi pumps.
  • In-Situ Machining: On-board refacing of sealing surfaces and line boring on brackets, stern tubes, and rudder tubes.
  • Propeller Maintenance: NDT (Ultrasonic/Dye penetrant) inspections, buffing, preservation, and tip straightening coordination.

Quality Assurance & Compliance

All work is performed in accordance with OEM procedures and classification society rules, under a multi-stage QA/QC process. Inspections are witnessed by OEM representatives, owners’ representatives, or classification agencies to ensure full transparency and traceability.

We undertake the repair, overhaul, and restoration of auxiliary equipment and the related piping systems that are critical to a vessel’s operation with full compliance with classification and OEM standards.

Piping Systems

Expert overhaul of systems including compressed air, fuel, bilge, ballast, fire-fighting, seawater, freshwater, sewage, chilled water, and exhaust systems. We work with CS, SS, CuNi, Copper, and Titanium metallurgies.

Our process includes spool-level drawing creation, chemical cleaning, and hydro/air pressure testing to locate and repair leaks. We also restore hot and cold insulation and install new cladding for exhaust systems.

Valve Reconditioning

We recondition, manufacture components, or replace all valve types. Every valve is pressure tested to a multiple of its safe working pressure in accordance with QA/QC procedures.

Auxiliary Equipment

Complete repair and testing for:

  • Pump Types: Triplex, centrifugal, gear pumps.
  • Air Compressors: Low- and high-pressure units.

Disassembled components are cleaned, inspected against OEM tolerances, and rebuilt with new seals, bearings, and wear rings.

Comprehensive repair and restoration services for marine boilers, economizers, and steam turbine-driven equipment to ensure safe and efficient power generation.

Boiler & Economizer Services

  • Tube Cleaning & Replacement: Mechanical and chemical cleaning; tube replacement with OEM-quality materials.
  • Welding & Hydrostatic Testing: Certified welding followed by hydrostatic pressure testing per approved QAPs.
  • Refractory Renewal: Full furnace refractory removal and renewal using heat-resistant linings.
  • Mountings Overhaul: Safety valves, shut-off devices, fuel sprayers, and soot blowers.

Steam Turbine-Driven Machinery

Full dismantling and overhaul of auxiliary machinery like Forced Draft Blowers, pumps, and generators. Includes dimensional inspection, chemical cleaning of oil coolers, thrust bearing inspection, and dynamic balancing of impellers.

Governor Setting & Calibration

Servicing and calibration of turbine governor systems, including adjustment of mechanical/hydraulic linkages and setting trip speeds/control limits.

From single-compartment ventilation units to fully integrated HVAC plants, we deliver class-compliant repair and restoration services.

Component Servicing

  • Compressors: Full dismantling, chemical cleaning, and rebuild with OEM spares (e.g., Bitzer).
  • Air Handling Units (AHUs): Evaporator coil cleaning, impeller balancing, and motor overhauls.
  • Chiller Pumps: Seal replacement, bearing renewal, and pressure testing.
  • Condensers & Chillers: Chemical cleaning and pressure testing of tubes.
  • Ventilation Fans: Casing repair, motor overvarnishing, and impeller balancing.
  • Ducting Systems: Robotic/manual cleaning; replacement of corroded sections and thermal insulation.

Control & Final Trials

Calibration of expansion valves, sensors, and thermostats. Final vacuum and pressure testing for refrigerant leak detection, followed by heat load tests to evaluate efficiency.

Proper surface preparation is key to long-lasting vessel protection. Our treatment capabilities include:

  • Grit Blasting: Mechanical cleaning for effective surface roughening.
  • Vacuum Blasting: Minimized airborne dust for cleaner, blast-profiled surfaces.
  • UHP Hydro Blasting: Ultra-High Pressure (up to 40K PSI) coating removal without abrasives.
  • Vacuum Shot Blasting: Specialized for flight decks and high-spec surface requirements.
  • HP Hydro Jetting: Pipe internal cleaning and tank scaling removal.

Our certified coating teams deliver specialized marine-grade finishes under the supervision of NACE-certified painting inspectors.

  • Epoxy-Based Coatings: High-durability corrosion protection.
  • Coal-Tar Epoxy: For submerged and high-corrosion zones.
  • Non-Skid Deck Coatings: Enhanced traction and crew safety for naval platforms.

All coatings are applied in strict accordance with paint OEM specifications and classification requirements for long-term protection in harsh marine environments.
