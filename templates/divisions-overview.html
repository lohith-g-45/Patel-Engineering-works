Divisions Overview

Delivering end-to-end technical solutions to India's defence, public-sector and commercial marine clients.

We operate across two core domains — Shipbuilding and Ship Repair & Modernization — delivering end-to-end technical solutions to India's defence, public-sector and commercial marine clients. Every scope is executed by class-qualified teams under strict QA/QC, in full compliance with class, OEM and naval standards.

Our Workshops & Infrastructure

Our strategic network of workshops, combined with our established presence at major shipyards and dockyards, lets us mobilise rapidly and execute with speed across key maritime regions. Each facility offers substantial indoor and outdoor work areas with integrated cranes for safe handling of heavy equipment.

Across these workshops we deliver, among much else:

  • New fabrication and structural repairs
  • Repair and overhaul of shafting, CPP propulsion units, thrusters, water jets and gearboxes
  • Overhaul of pumps (triplex, centrifugal, gear) and compressors
  • Hydraulic power packs and cylinders; motors, blowers and control panels
  • All types of coolers and valves
  • Fabrication and repair of piping in copper, cupro-nickel, stainless steel, mild steel and titanium

A Complete Supporting Ecosystem

Our workshops are backed by an ecosystem that lets us deliver complete, in-house solutions — reducing dependencies and turnaround time:

  • Precision dynamic balancing
  • On-site ferrous and non-ferrous casting
  • Advanced CNC machining
  • Metal build-up and rebabbitting
  • NABL-approved testing and NDT laboratories
  • Independent third-party inspection services
  • Storage and maintenance of our extensive fleet of abrasive and UHP blasting equipment

Financial Strength

Our proven record as a reliable solution provider and trusted partner — particularly within the naval defence sector — has built strong client confidence and allowed us to steadily expand and diversify both our revenue and our client base.

  • Consistent growth with healthy margins, reflecting our market strength and operational efficiency
  • Balanced revenue across shipbuilding and ship repair, for stability and resilience
  • A strong national client presence across India
  • An independent credit rating affirming our financial stability and ISO 9001 quality certification

This financial strength underpins our capacity to manage large turnkey projects reliably — maintaining healthy cash flows for timely payments to our vendor and OEM partners and continuous investment in robotics, equipment and our people.
