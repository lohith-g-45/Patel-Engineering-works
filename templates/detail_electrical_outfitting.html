Powering Performance at Sea

We specialize in the complete execution of Marine Electrical Outfitting works, delivering end-to-end installation, integration, and commissioning support strictly as per client-provided designs and Technical documentation. As a trusted contractor to leading Shipyards and OEMs, we bring proven expertise in executing complex electrical scopes on both Naval and Commercial Vessels"with a strong track record of on-time delivery and Quality workmanship.

Our workforce is comprised of highly skilled and certified electricians and technicians, supported by experienced supervisors and project engineers who ensure Technical compliance, efficient coordination, and adherence to project timelines. Their combined expertise allows us to deliver precision installations, even in high-complexity zones such as engine rooms, control centres, and combat system areas.

Our teams are trained to handle both low- and high-voltage Marine systems and are familiar with a wide range of cable types, termination practices, and electrical interfaces.
