Collaborating for excellence and expanding capabilities in the maritime sector.

To cater to the growing demand for Pressfit Systems in Indian Navy, M/s PEW(V) collaborated with M/s Raccorderie Metalliche (RM), Italy, a 55 year old company and a leading manufacturer and supplier of Pressfit pipe-fittings system across the globe.

RM pressfit systems are in use by many major shipyards and navies across the globe. One of their biggest client is M/s Fincantieri Shipyard, who have been using their pressfit systems for their frontline warships.

PEW(V) along with RM introduced their products to Indian Navy in Sep 2023. After successfull completion of all the tests (including NSTL Shock and Vibration) and approval of all drawings, we were approved as a potential supplier and installer of our products on Naval Warships in Apr 2025.
