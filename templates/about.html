About Our Company

A trusted technical partner to naval and commercial shipyards for shipbuilding, repair, and lifecycle support.

Founded in 2001, Patel Engineering Works (Vizag) is headed by Mr. Rashesh Sanghvi and Mr. Chirag Sanghvi, with Mr. Kiran Sanghvi serving as the current CEO. We boast a robust legacy with an executive and supervisory team that brings many decades of collective experience in ship repairs and shipbuilding.

Our operations are primarily focused on serving as a trusted partner to leading shipyards and ship repair yards to deliver comprehensive technical services and project management for the construction of new vessels and the repair and refit of existing ships.

With a dedicated workforce of over 860 skilled professionals across India, including engineers, qualified supervisors, and technical staff, we specialize in major ship repair, shipbuilding, and maintenance activities at key Indian ports such as Mumbai, Visakhapatnam, Goa, Cochin, Karwar, Kolkata, and Port Blair, as well as Colombo, Sri Lanka.

We take pride in serving prestigious clients such as the Indian Navy, Indian Coast Guard, Hindustan Shipyard, Goa Shipyard, Cochin Shipyard, Garden Reach Shipbuilders and Engineers, Mazagon Dockyard, L&T Shipbuilding Chennai, Naval Science and Technological Laboratory (NSTL), and select rig and ship owners.

Our unwavering commitment to quality, safety, and efficiency has earned us the trust of naval defense and public sector undertakings.

With sustained growth over decades and a steadfast dedication to excellence, Patel Engineering Works (Vizag) is poised for continued success, ready to undertake large turnkey projects and contribute to the evolving naval defense and maritime landscape.
