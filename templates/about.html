About Our Company

A trusted Technical partner to Naval and Commercial Shipyards for Shipbuilding, repair, and lifecycle support.

Founded in 2001, Patel Engineering Works (Vizag) is a trusted partner to leading Shipyards, specializing in Shipbuilding, repairs, Refits, and Maintenance. Led by experienced promoters and a seasoned Leadership, the company delivers comprehensive Technical and project management solutions.

With a workforce of 860+ skilled professionals deployed across major Indian ports and Colombo, we support both new Vessel construction and complex repair projects. We proudly serve prestigious clients including the Indian Navy, Indian Coast Guard, and leading public and private Shipyards.

Driven by a strong commitment to Quality, Safety, and efficiency, Patel Engineering Works continues to grow as a reliable partner in India’s Naval Defense and Maritime industry, with the capability to execute large turnkey projects.
