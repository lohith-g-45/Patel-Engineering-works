As an experienced Marine Engineering contractor, Patel Engineering Works (Vizag) specializes in the systematic renewal and structural repair of corroded or damaged ship steelwork - both afloat and in drydock conditions.

Structural Scope

We carry out extensive Steel Renewal for Hull plating, internal bulkheads, tank structures, deck houses, and other key load-bearing and watertight components. Our services are tailored to meet the demanding requirements of Naval and Commercial platforms, where precision, speed, and classification compliance are non-negotiable.

Certified Technical Teams

Our Steel Renewal teams are composed of highly skilled fabricators and ABS/IRS-certified Welders, proficient in working under tight schedules, complex geometries, and Onboard constraints. All work is performed in strict adherence to approved Welding Procedure Specifications (WPS), with materials sourced as per IACS, Indian Navy, or client-specific standards.

Advanced Welding Techniques

  • FCAW - Flux-Cored Arc Welding
  • SMAW - Shielded Metal Arc Welding
  • GMAW - Gas Metal Arc Welding
  • GTAW - Gas Tungsten Arc Welding

Specialized Materials

We routinely work with IS 2062 for standard Steel Renewal, AH36 for high-strength applications, and DMR 249A for Naval-grade requirements. Our teams are also well-versed in fabricating and repairing structures made from aluminium alloy H8053 H111, commonly used for lightweight Naval superstructures.

Our stringent QA/QC protocols are overseen by NDT Level II certified inspectors who ensure weld integrity and dimensional conformity at every stage of renewal.
