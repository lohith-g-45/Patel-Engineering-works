Leadership

Driving Patel Engineering Works Toward Excellence in Ship Repair and Shipbuilding.

At Patel Engineering Works (vizag), Our Leadership is the Cornerstone of Our Success. Our Management Team Combines Decades of Hands on Industry Experience with Strong Business Acumen, Driving the Company Toward Excellence in Ship Repair and Shipbuilding.

Our Founders

Patel Engineering Works (vizag) is Headed by Mr Rashesh Sanghvi and Mr Chirag Sanghvi, with Mr Kiran Sanghvi as Founder and CEO. Our Journey in the Maritime Industry Began from the Ground Up Working Shoulder to Shoulder with Project Teams, Supervisors and Skilled Tradesmen. That Hands on Grounding Shaped a Deep Understanding of Both the Technical and Operational Sides of Shipbuilding and Repair and It Still Defines How the Firm is Run Today.

Mr Kiran Sanghvi

Founder and Chief Executive Officer

At the Helm of Our Strategic Leadership is Mr Kiran Sanghvi, Who Serves as Both Mentor and Strategist for the Firm. With a Diploma in Electrical Engineering and Over 40 Years of Hands on Experience in the Maritime Industry, He Has Worked with Some of the Most Prestigious Names in the Field:

  • MAN B&w — Engines & Propulsion Systems
  • Norwinch — Low Pressure Hydraulic Windlasses, Mooring Winches & Steering Gear
  • Hatlappa — High Pressure Hydraulic Windlasses, Mooring Winches, Steering Gear & Compressors
  • Ascargo, Spain — Hatch Covers, Bow Doors & Ramp Doors

His Technical Expertise, Paired with a Genuine Passion for Innovation, Has Been Instrumental in Driving the Company's Growth and Its Reputation for Excellence.

Mr Rashesh Sanghvi

Director Strategy and Client Leadership

With Over 25 Years of Experience, Mr Rashesh Sanghvi Has Helped Shape the Company's Strategic Direction. His Strengths Lie in Client Relationship Management, Project Oversight and Resolving HR and Logistical Challenges, Ensuring Smooth Execution and Strong, Lasting Client Partnerships.

Mr Chirag Sanghvi

Partner Business Development and Projects

Bringing 15 Years of Industry Experience, Mr Chirag Sanghvi Leads Business Development, Project Management and Costing & Bidding. His Approach to Project Strategy and Growth Has Been Pivotal in Securing Major Contracts and Expanding the Firm's Presence Across the Marine Industry.

Our Management Philosophy

Our Leadership Operates on Collaboration, Flexibility and Consensus Driven Decision Making. While the Team Shares Overlapping Responsibilities, Every Project is Led by a Designated Project Manager So the Most Relevant Skills are Applied to Each Job.

  • Project Leadership: Roles are Assigned Dynamically to Each Project's Unique Requirements, for Optimal Use of Expertise
  • Consensus Based Decisions: Major Calls, Including Bidding, are Made Through Full Consensus Across the CEO, Partners and Project Managers

Above All, Our Leadership is Committed to Innovation, the Highest Standards of Quality and Safety and Long Term Relationships with Clients and Partners.
