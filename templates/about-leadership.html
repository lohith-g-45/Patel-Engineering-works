Leadership

Our leadership drives our success. With decades of hands-on industry experience and sharp business acumen, our management team champions excellence in shipbuilding, repair, and maritime solutions

Our CEO - Mr. Kiran Sanghvi

Founder and Chief Executive Officer

With a Diploma in Electrical Engineering and over 40 years of hands-on experience, Mr. Kiran Sanghvi serves as both mentor and strategist for the firm. His technical expertise and passion for innovation have been instrumental in the company’s growth and reputation for excellence.

Mr. Rashesh Sanghvi (B.Com)

Partner - Strategy and Client Leadership

With over 25 years of experience, Mr. Rashesh Sanghvi has played a key role in shaping the company’s strategic direction. His strengths include client relationship management, project oversight, and resolving HR and logistical challenges for smooth project execution.

Mr. Chirag Sanghvi (B.S. Industrial Engineering, MBA)

Partner - Business Development and Projects

Bringing 15 years of industry experience, Mr. Chirag Sanghvi leads business development, project management, and costing and bidding. His innovative approach has been pivotal in securing major contracts and expanding the firm’s presence in the marine sector.
