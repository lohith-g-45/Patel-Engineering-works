We deliver a focused suite of services for the Propulsion and steering (stern gear equipment) of Naval and Commercial Vessels, ensuring smooth maneuverability and long-term reliability.

Core Capabilities

  • Routine Maintenance & Overhaul: Scheduled servicing of steering systems, rudder assemblies, and complete shaft lines.
  • Heavy Lift Operations: Custom lifting frames and mobile crane operations safely extract/reinstall heavy assemblies.
  • Laser Shaft Alignment: Precision verification from main engine output to propeller flange within stringent tolerances.
  • Propulsion Systems: Fixed Pitch or Controllable Pitch (CPP), Azimuth/Tunnel thrusters, and Waterjets.
  • Deep Seal Systems: Inspection and complete replacement of seal arrangements.
  • Gearboxes & Clutches: Overhaul and repair of reduction gearboxes, clutches, PTO units, and FiFi pumps.
  • In-Situ Machining: On-board refacing of sealing surfaces and line boring on stern tubes and rudder tubes.
  • Propeller Maintenance: NDT inspections, buffing, preservation, and tip straightening coordination.

Quality Assurance & Compliance

All work is performed in accordance with OEM procedures and classification society rules. Inspections are witnessed by OEM representatives, owners' representatives, or classification agencies to ensure full transparency and traceability.
