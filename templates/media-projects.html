Project Highlights

Delivering precision Engineering and turnkey execution for critical Defense vessels.

INS Car Nicobar – Major Refit

Naval Dockyard, Visakhapatnam · 6-month Major Refit

The first ever major refit of INS car nicobar — and the first time a private firm was trusted with a major refit of this fast attack craft class. Delivered on time to the Indian navy's exacting standards, extending the vessel's operational life for years to come.

INS Kalpeni – Major Refit

GRSE / Naval Shipyard Kochi · MoU partner to GRSE

The maiden major refit of INS kalpeni, a fast attack craft — the first WJFAC class vessel refitted on a turnkey basis at GRSE, completed on schedule.

INS Astradharini – Short Refit (SR 25)

Hindustan Shipyard Limited · Completed November 2025 · On time

A repeat refit of this specialized naval vessel — a platform we have supported through multiple cycles. Scope covered overhaul and renewal of pumps, valves, piping, motors, control panels, STP and EVAC systems, fuel oil centrifuge, starting compressors, davits and flood/fire detection systems, plus hull upkeep, deck machinery servicing and propeller replacement. Recognised with appreciation from the commanding officer and crew.

Representative image — photography restricted for this vessel

BPT 2 Battle Practice Target Barge – Special Repair

Naval Dockyard, Visakhapatnam · Special Repair

Restoration of a heavily used naval target/training platform to full working order — extensive hull repairs, overhaul of onboard systems and deck machinery, diesel generator overhaul and replacement, a new LCP, main switchboard and inverter and renewal of power, control and lighting cables across the vessel, including procurement and installation of mission critical equipment.

SCGS Zoroaster – Short Refit

Seychelles Coast Guard, at GRSE · Completed in 2 months

An international refit delivered within schedule while running parallel assignments — covering main engines, diesel alternators, the engine control system, waterjets, electrical systems, pumps, compressors, valves and life saving equipment, plus refurbishment of the wardroom and captain's cabin.

From turnkey new builds to frontline warship support, we deliver complex shipbuilding scopes to class and naval standards. A selection of landmark work:

Six 10T Tugs — Indian Navy

Hindustan Shipyard Ltd · Full design-to-delivery

Complete turnkey construction of six 10 tonne tugs under a full design to delivery framework — basic design, model testing and production drawings with class and Indian Navy approvals, hull fabrication and erection, installation and commissioning of main engines and schottel propulsion, electrical and accommodation outfitting and piping. All six were undocked simultaneously and delivered to their home ports — a landmark that established our turnkey credentials.

Next Generation Electric Ferry

Garden Reach Shipbuilders & Engineers · 2023–24

A catamaran type, solar electric propulsion ferry built for GRSE and delivered to the government of west bengal as part of its inland waterway modernization — showcasing our capability in clean propulsion and composite vessel construction.

Six 13m FRP Patrol Boats

With GRSE · Bangladesh Fisheries Department

Turnkey construction of six 13 metre FRP patrol boats, delivered on schedule — deepening our expertise in FRP composite vessels and end to end project delivery, from design and procurement to commissioning.

Frontline Warship Construction at GRSE

Garden Reach Shipbuilders & Engineers · 600+ personnel

A long standing shipbuilding partner at GRSE, contributing a significant share of hull fabrication and erection, piping and electrical outfitting across some of the navy's most advanced platforms — P17A frigates, anti submarine warfare corvettes, offshore patrol vessels, water jet fast attack craft, landing craft utility vessels and survey vessels.

Machinery Installation at HSL

Hindustan Shipyard Ltd

Installation and commissioning of propulsion and machinery across larger platforms — voith and schottel propulsion on 50T and 25T tugs, shafting and propulsion alignment plus accommodation outfitting on 53,000 DWT bulk carriers and bow thruster installation on diving support vessels.

Building on decades of Marine Engineering, we are investing in the technologies that will shape the next era of naval and commercial shipbuilding:

Press Fit Piping Systems

Partnership with Raccorderie Metalliche (Italy)

In partnership with raccorderie metalliche (italy), a 55 year global leader, we have introduced modern press fit piping to the Indian Navy — faster to install, easier to maintain and more reliable than conventional systems. Following naval drawing approval and successful NSTL shock & vibration testing, PEWV serves as the authorized distribution and onboard installation partner for naval warships. Already proven in service, including the fresh water system upgrade aboard SCGS zoroaster.

Unmanned Surface Vessels (usvs)

Prototype Development

Leveraging our experience in high speed FRP and aluminium vessels, we have initiated development of prototype usvs featuring advanced propulsion and autonomy systems — positioning PEWV at the forefront of next generation maritime platforms.

Robotics & Automation

Technology Investment

We continue to invest in robotics for surface preparation and confined space work — including robotic systems for hazardous environments.
