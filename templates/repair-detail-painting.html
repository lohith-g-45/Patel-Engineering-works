Our certified coating teams deliver specialized Marine-grade finishes under the supervision of NACE-certified Painting inspectors, ensuring all work meets OEM and classification society standards.

Coating Systems

  • Epoxy-Based Coatings: High-durability corrosion protection for Hull interiors, tanks, and structural surfaces.
  • Coal-Tar Epoxy: Superior protection for submerged zones and high-corrosion areas including bilges and ballast tanks.
  • Non-Skid Deck Coatings: Enhanced traction and crew Safety for flight decks and exposed areas on Naval platforms.
  • Anti-Fouling Systems: Biocide-based and foul-release systems for Hull underwater body protection.
  • Intumescent Coatings: Passive fire protection for structural steel compartments.

Application Standards

All coatings are applied in strict accordance with paint OEM specifications and classification requirements. Each coat is applied at the specified dry film thickness (DFT) and verified by certified inspectors using calibrated DFT gauges. Holiday testing is performed on critical areas to confirm coating continuity.

NACE-Supervised Inspection

Our NACE-certified Painting inspectors oversee every stage from Surface Preparation acceptance to final coat sign-off, providing complete documentation and Inspection records for classification submission.
