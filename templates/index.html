Project Highlights

INS Car Nicobar

The first-ever Major Refit of INS Car Nicobar — and the first time a private firm was trusted with a Major Refit of this Fast Attack Craft class. Delivered on time to the Indian Navy's exacting standards, extending the vessel's operational life for years to come.

INS Kalpeni

The maiden Major Refit of INS Kalpeni, a Fast Attack Craft — the first WJFAC-class vessel refitted on a turnkey basis at GRSE, completed on schedule.

INS Astradharini

A repeat refit of this specialized naval vessel — a platform we have supported through multiple cycles. Scope covered overhaul and renewal of auxiliary machinery, piping systems, and deck upkeep.
