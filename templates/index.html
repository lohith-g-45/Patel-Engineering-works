About PEW Vizag

Specialized shipbuilding and ship repair solutions with proven delivery across defense and commercial maritime sectors

Patel Engineering Works (Vizag) is a leading provider of specialized shipbuilding and ship repair services, supporting the Indian Navy, Coast Guard, public-sector shipyards, and select international clients. With over two decades of proven execution and a skilled workforce deployed across India, we bring unmatched technical depth and project reliability to every engagement.

Our shipbuilding division delivers high-integrity fabrication, outfitting, and equipment installation services for complex defense and auxiliary vessels, working in close alignment with classification societies and OEMs.

Parallelly, our ship repair services division undertakes turnkey refits, emergency repairs, and precision overhauls for a wide range of onboard systems, ensuring operational readiness with minimal downtime.

Known for our rigorous quality systems, certified personnel, and timely project completion, we are committed to delivering consistent results across both new construction and lifecycle support. Whether executing at yard, onboard, or within our dedicated workshops, we bring the same focus: technical excellence, safety, and dependable service.

  • Shipbuilding & Repair

    Strong capabilities across new-build support, refits, and lifecycle services

  • Dependable Delivery

    On-time completion with safety-first execution across yard and onboard work
