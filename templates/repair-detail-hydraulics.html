Patel Engineering Works (Vizag) specializes in the comprehensive Overhaul and testing of hydraulic systems on Naval and Commercial Vessels - including steering gear, deck machinery (winches, cranes), bow and stern doors, and specialized torpedo-launching mechanisms.

Technical Scope

Our scope covers high-pressure hydraulic lines, cylinders, valves, pumps, motors, and control systems - each subjected to pressure testing, full-system flushing, and leak-detection protocols to guarantee operational integrity. We also bench-test pumps and motors post-Overhaul to verify performance before reinstallation.

Precision Restoration

Our technicians replace flexible hoses and fabricate Piping assemblies with classification-approved fittings, adhering to ISO 28521 cleanliness standards. Hydraulic cylinders are either reconditioned via precision grinding, seal renewal, and hard-chrome plating, or manufactured new to OEM specifications.

Rigorous QA/QC

We perform hydrostatic tests at 1.5 x design pressure, functional valve calibration, and final system tuning to confirm leak-free operation and compliance with OEM and class-society standards.
