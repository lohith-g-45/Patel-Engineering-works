From single-compartment ventilation units to fully integrated Hvac plants, we deliver class-compliant repair and restoration services.

Component Servicing

  • Compressors: Full dismantling, chemical cleaning, and rebuild with OEM spares (e.g., Bitzer).
  • Air Handling Units (AHUs): Evaporator coil cleaning, impeller balancing, and motor Overhauls.
  • Chiller Pumps: Seal replacement, bearing renewal, and pressure testing.
  • Condensers & Chillers: Chemical cleaning and pressure testing of tubes.
  • Ventilation Fans: Casing repair, motor overvarnishing, and impeller balancing.
  • Ducting Systems: Robotic/manual cleaning; replacement of corroded sections and thermal insulation.

Control & Final Trials

Calibration of expansion valves, sensors, and thermostats. Final vacuum and pressure testing for refrigerant leak detection, followed by heat load tests to evaluate efficiency.
