In-Situ Machining

In-Situ Machining

Restoring Accuracy and Enabling Precision in Shipbuilding

Welding during ship construction—particularly in thick structural members—can often cause thermal distortion, misalignment, and surface irregularities that compromise the precise geometry required for critical equipment installation. Our in-situ machining services are designed to restore this dimensional accuracy directly onboard during the shipbuilding process, helping to keep construction schedules on track.

These services play a crucial role in ensuring precision alignment and surface preparation at various stages of ship construction. By enabling machining directly at the site of installation, we support the integration of mechanical, structural, and mission-critical systems with the exacting tolerances demanded by naval and commercial vessels.

Through these capabilities, we ensure system reliability, seamless integration, and alignment across all critical assemblies—supporting faster commissioning, long-term performance, and construction quality that consistently meets or exceeds naval and classification society standards.

Advanced Machining Operations

Our experienced machinists, equipped with advanced portable machining systems, routinely perform operations in full compliance with OEM specifications and shipyard tolerances:

→ Line boring & flange facing
→ Precision milling
→ Piano wire alignment
→ Laser measurement calibration

Key Installation Support

We carry out specialized in-situ machining for the exact installation and foundation of critical shipboard components:

→ Stern gear components
→ Hinge pins for watertight doors
→ Thrusters & propulsion foundations
→ Radars and sonar domes
→ Gun mounts and weapon systems
