Piping & Plumbing Systems

Comprehensive Marine Engineering Solutions for Naval, Offshore, and Commercial Vessel programs.

Fabrication & Installation of Plumbing/Pipeline Systems

Precision and Reliability at Every Stage

Our expertise in piping and Plumbing Fabrication and erection is a critical element in enabling our Shipyard clients to deliver fully integrated, seaworthy Vessels that meet the exacting standards of Naval and Commercial Marine operations.

We undertake end-to-end Fabrication and installation of Marine piping systems, covering all essential utilities"compressed air, fuel, bilge, ballast, fire-fighting, seawater, fresh water, sewage, chilled water, exhaust, and other shipboard services. Whether Outfitting complex warships or high-performance Commercial craft, our teams deliver systems engineered to perform reliably under extreme operational conditions.

All Fabrication is carried out in compliance with the standards of leading classification societies such as IRS, ABS, DNV, among others. We adhere strictly to approved Quality Assurance Plans (QAPs), which include weld Inspection, pressure testing, and hydrotesting"both at the workshop level and post-erection Onboard.

Our processes are underpinned by detailed isometric drawing reviews and Bill of Materials (BoM) verification, supported by a robust QA/QC framework. Our in-house QC professionals ensure all installations are leak-proof, vibration-resistant, and compliant with all Technical specifications.

Advanced piping Solutions

In addition to welded systems, we fabricate pipelines using a range of mechanically attached fittings, such as Ermeto and press-fit solutions, based on project needs.

Complementing our piping services, we also offer both cold and hot insulation, along with cladding for thermal protection and acoustic performance.

From engine rooms to Accommodation areas, our piping and Plumbing solutions are built on Quality, precision, and a proven track record of on-time delivery"backed by decades of Shipbuilding expertise.

Material & Joint Configurations

Our pipefitters and Welders are fully trained and qualified to work with a wide range of materials and joint configurations, including:
