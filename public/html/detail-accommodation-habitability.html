Accommodation & Habitability Outfitting

Enhancing Crew Comfort and Operational Readiness

Modern Naval and Commercial Vessels demand not only Technical excellence but also superior crew Accommodation to support long deployments and ensure Onboard efficiency. Our Accommodation & Habitability Outfitting services are designed to deliver functional, durable, and ergonomic living environments that meet the highest standards of Maritime Habitability.

We specialize in the complete Outfitting of crew spaces � including living quarters, mess areas, galleys, sanitary blocks, medical compartments, and executive cabins � executed as an integral part of ship construction. These operations are closely coordinated with parallel Shipyard activities to ensure smooth integration and adherence to construction timelines.

Our scope begins at the design stage, where we collaborate with the Shipyard or end client where possible to develop tailored layouts, furniture configurations, and material schemes. All designs are submitted for client approval prior to production, ensuring complete alignment with operational and aesthetic expectations.

In addition to Outfitting, our teams carry out lighting, electrical, and Plumbing works specifically suited for Accommodation compartments, ensuring a fully integrated and operational living environment. We also supply, install, and commission all galley equipment along with the galley fire-fighting systems, delivering a fully functional and Safety-compliant culinary space Onboard.

Our Outfitting teams are recognized for their speed, efficiency, and high attention to detail. Every space we deliver reflects our commitment to Quality, crew comfort, and Shipyard coordination � supporting operational excellence on both Naval and Commercial platforms.

Furniture & Finishes

We procure and install high-Quality CRCA powdered coated furniture for workspaces and general living quarters, and stainless-steel furniture for dining halls, galley spaces, and medical compartments. For wardrooms and executive areas, we offer premium woodwork with Marine-grade finishes, aesthetic wall treatments, and specialized Marine flooring.

→ Marine-grade CRCA & stainless-steel furniture
→ Custom woodwork and veneering
→ Modular wall & acoustic ceiling panels
→ Marine flooring & deck underlay treatments
→ FR fabrics for curtains & upholstery

Integrated Compartment Utilities

Where required, fire-retardant (FR) fabrics are used to meet stringent Naval and class Safety norms. We complete every compartment with fully integrated supporting utilities:

→ Sanitary systems & freshwater piping
→ Accommodation lighting & electrical integration
→ Complete Plumbing & waste connections
→ Galley equipment supply & commissioning
→ Galley fire-fighting system installation
→ Client-approved design execution
