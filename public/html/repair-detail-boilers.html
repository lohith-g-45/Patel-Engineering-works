Boilers & Steam Turbine-Driven Equipment

Precision repair of thermal power systems for safe and efficient power generation.

Comprehensive repair and restoration services for Marine Boilers, economizers, and steam turbine-driven equipment to ensure safe and efficient power generation.

Boiler & Economizer Services

  • Tube Cleaning & Replacement: Mechanical and chemical cleaning; tube replacement with OEM-Quality materials.
  • Welding & Hydrostatic Testing: Certified Welding followed by hydrostatic pressure testing per approved QAPs.
  • Refractory Renewal: Full furnace refractory removal and renewal using heat-resistant linings.
  • Mountings Overhaul: Safety valves, shut-off devices, fuel sprayers, and soot blowers.

Steam Turbine-Driven Machinery

Full dismantling and Overhaul of Auxiliary machinery like Forced Draft Blowers, pumps, and generators. Includes dimensional Inspection, chemical cleaning of oil coolers, thrust bearing Inspection, and dynamic balancing of impellers.

Governor Setting & Calibration

Servicing and calibration of turbine governor systems, including adjustment of mechanical/hydraulic linkages and setting trip speeds/control limits.
