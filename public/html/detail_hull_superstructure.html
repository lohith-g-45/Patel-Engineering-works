Hull & Superstructure Fabrication

Our Core Strength in Shipbuilding

As a highly experienced shipbuilding company, our core strength lies in the comprehensive fabrication and erection of hulls and superstructures, enabling us to craft entire vessels from the keel up.

We specialize in the execution of complex shipbuilding projects, particularly the specialized platforms such as the Indian Navy, Coast Guard, and other entities requiring technically demanding vessels.

Certified & Skilled Workforce

Our team of highly skilled welders is rigorously qualified and certified by leading international classification societies, including IRS, ABS, and DNV. To ensure consistently superior workmanship, they undergo continuous training and re-certification in the required welding techniques and standards.

Our stringent quality control processes are reinforced by NDT Level II certified inspectors who meticulously ensure all welds meet project specifications.

Advanced Welding Techniques

→ FCAW (Flux-Cored Arc Welding)
→ SMAW (Shielded Metal Arc Welding)
→ GMAW (Gas Metal Arc Welding)
→ GTAW (Gas Tungsten Arc Welding)
→ SAW (Submerged Arc Welding)

Material Expertise

Our expertise extends to working with a diverse range of materials, including:

→ Standard grades such as IS 2062
→ Non-standard high-strength grades like AH36
→ Specialized naval-grade steel such as DMR 249A (naval specifications)
→ Aluminium alloy H8053 H111 for lightweight, high-performance marine applications
