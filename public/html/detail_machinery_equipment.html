Machinery & Equipment Installation

Machinery and Equipment Installation

Precision, Power, and Performance

We offer comprehensive equipment installation services across all major shipboard systems, with proven expertise in handling critical equipment in both deck and engine room spaces. Working closely with shipyards and OEMs, we execute installations strictly as per technical drawings and specifications—with a strong commitment to precision, quality, and on-time delivery.

We specialize in the installation and alignment of propulsion chains, including shaft lines and stern gear, using advanced laser alignment tools and techniques to ensure precision. This guarantees reduced vibration, optimal performance, and long-term durability of propulsion systems.

Our team of skilled mechanical fitters, riggers, and experienced supervisors, supported by qualified project engineers, ensures safe handling, accurate installation, and seamless mechanical interfacing. We carry out chocking, grouting, alignment verification, and support all stages of pre-commissioning and functional testing.

All installations adhere to the standards of leading classification societies such as IRS, ABS, DNV, and are executed in line with detailed QA/QC plans provided by the shipyard or OEM. We also provide hands-on support during harbor and sea trials, ensuring every system meets operational expectations before handover.

Scope of Installation & Integration

→ Deck Machinery
Windlasses, capstans, mooring winches, davits, cranes, and hydraulic handling systems
→ Engine Room Equipment
Main propulsion engines, gearboxes, shaft couplings, generators, oily water separators (OWS), pumps, RO plants, compressors, and heat exchangers
→ Control & Maneuvering Systems
Steering gear units (ram-type, rotary vane, and electro-hydraulic systems), rudder stocks, and hydraulic power packs

Continued

→ Stability and Control Systems
Fin stabilizers and associated control units
→ Propulsion Systems
Shaft lines, stern gear, controllable pitch propellers (CPP), azimuth and tunnel thrusters, and waterjet propulsion units
→ Specialized Systems
Bow doors, cargo access ramps, aircraft and equipment elevators, including mechanical, hydraulic, and electro-hydraulic systems

Whether it’s deck equipment, complex engine room machinery, precision propulsion assemblies, or mission-critical access and lifting systems like bow doors and elevators, we deliver mechanical installation solutions that are reliable, accurate, and completed on time—trusted by top shipyards and OEMs.
