Piping, Valves, Pumps & Compressions

Restoring vital shipboard Piping, valves, pumps, and compressor systems.

We undertake the repair, Overhaul, and restoration of Auxiliary equipment and related Piping systems that are critical to a Vessel's operation, with full compliance with classification and OEM standards.

Piping Systems

Expert Overhaul of systems including compressed air, fuel, bilge, ballast, fire-fighting, seawater, freshwater, sewage, chilled water, and exhaust systems. We work with CS, SS, CuNi, Copper, and Titanium metallurgies.

Our process includes spool-level drawing creation, chemical cleaning, and hydro/air pressure testing to locate and repair leaks. We also restore hot and cold insulation and install new cladding for exhaust systems.

Valve Reconditioning

We recondition, manufacture components, or replace all valve types. Every valve is pressure tested to a multiple of its safe working pressure in accordance with QA/QC procedures.

Auxiliary Equipment

  • Pump Types: Triplex, centrifugal, gear pumps.
  • Air Compressors: Low- and high-pressure units.

Disassembled components are cleaned, inspected against OEM tolerances, and rebuilt with new seals, bearings, and wear rings.
